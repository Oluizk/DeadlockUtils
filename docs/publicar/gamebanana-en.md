Deadlock Utils is an in-game HUD mod made with Panorama, the game's own UI. No overlay and no external app: everything opens inside the game and follows the game's look.

What it does

• Shop tab: a new tab in the shop (B) with your hero's build for the current phase (popularity data shipped with the game), counters to the enemy team and the dangerous items enemies bought. It uses the shop's own item cards, so the tooltip and click to buy work like the rest of the shop. Hover an item to see why it was recommended.
• Items on Tab: hold Tab and two short rows show up above the investment circles: Counters (with the portrait of who it counters) and Recommended. Items you own disappear, items you cannot buy right now are faded. Click to buy.
• Big map (M): the game's own minimap, big, with free cursor and pings. Bridge buff and mid boss timers, plus points of interest you can toggle: healing snacks, bells, jump pads, golden statues, crates, cosmic veils, teleporters, shops, soul urn, neutral camps and Rem's tunnels (read from the game's map files). Hover a point to see what it is, click to mark it.
• Minimap points: the same points on the regular minimap, with their own filters and small icons.
• Tracker (F6 or ESC menu): rank, most played heroes, performance on the current hero and your history with or against each player (deadlock-api.com).
• Dangerous item alert when an enemy buys Knockdown, Cursed Relic, Refresher and similar items.
• Counter editor: edit the counters for any hero in game and share them with a short code.
• Settings: map and minimap sizes, icon sizes, filters, hotkeys.
• Portuguese, English and Spanish.

Install

1. Close Deadlock.
2. Extract the zip and run Instalar.bat. It finds the game, enables the addons folder in gameinfo.gi (with a backup) and copies the mod.
3. For the tracker: shop (B) > Deadlock Utils > Settings > Tracker, enter your Steam friend code (the installer shows it).

Run the installer again after Deadlock updates. To uninstall, run Desinstalar.bat. Manual install steps are in LEIA-ME.txt.

Notes

• It replaces the game's hud.xml, so it does not work together with other mods that replace it.
• Valve has not published rules about mods. HUD mods have not led to bans so far, but use it at your own risk.
• Open source, contributions welcome (counters, translations, map data): https://github.com/Oluizk/DeadlockUtils
