Deadlock Utils is an in-game HUD mod made with Panorama, the game's own UI: no overlay and no external app, everything opens inside the match with the game's look. It shows your hero's build and the counters to the enemy team in the shop, recommended items on Tab, a big map with timers and points of interest, and a player tracker. Available in English (default), Português (pt-BR), Español and Русский.

FeatureHow to useWhat it does
Shop tabShop (B) > Deadlock Utils tabYour hero's build for the current phase, counters to the enemy team and the dangerous items enemies bought, on the shop's own item cards. Hover to see why, click to buy.
Items on TabHold TabCounters and Recommended above the investment circles. Owned items disappear, items you can't buy right now are faded. Click to buy.
Big mapMThe game's minimap, big, with free cursor and pings. Bridge buff and mid boss timers, points of interest with filters (healing, bells, jump pads, statues, crates, veils, teleporters, shops, urn, camps, Rem's tunnels). Click a point to mark it.
Minimap pointsSettings > Map points > MiniThe same points on the regular minimap.
TrackerF6 or ESC menuRank, most played heroes and your history with or against each player (deadlock-api.com).
Danger alertAutomaticWarning when an enemy buys Knockdown, Cursed Relic, Refresher and similar items.
Counter editorShop tab > Edit countersEdit the counters for any hero and share them with a short code (DU1:...).
SettingsShop tab > SettingsMap and minimap sizes, icon sizes, filters, hotkeys. F8 turns the mod on/off.

Install

  • Mod manager (easiest): click the 1-click install button above (Deadlock Mod Manager, Grimoire, HELLCAT) and launch the game from the manager.
  • Manually: copy pak42_dir.vpk to Deadlock\game\citadel\addons\ and enable the addons folder in gameinfo.gi (steps in the README.txt inside the zip). To remove, delete the file.

Language: English by default. Change it in shop (B) > Deadlock Utils > Settings > Language (English, Português, Español, Русский). It switches right away.

Tracker: Settings > Tracker, enter your Steam friend code (Steam > Friends > Add a Friend).

Notes: it replaces the game's hud.xml, so it does not work with other mods that also replace it. Valve has no published rules about mods: use at your own risk. Open source: github.com/Oluizk/DeadlockUtils

Deadlock Utils é um mod de HUD feito em Panorama, a interface do próprio jogo: sem overlay e sem programa externo, tudo abre dentro da partida com o visual do jogo. Mostra na loja a build do seu herói e os counters contra o time inimigo, itens recomendados no Tab, um mapa grande com timers e pontos de interesse, e um tracker de jogadores. Disponível em inglês (padrão), português (pt-BR), espanhol e russo.

FunçãoComo usarO que faz

Instalar

  • Gerenciador de mods (mais fácil): clique no botão de instalação em 1 clique acima (Deadlock Mod Manager, Grimoire, HELLCAT) e abra o jogo pelo gerenciador.
  • Na mão: copie o pak42_dir.vpk para Deadlock\game\citadel\addons\ e habilite a pasta de addons no gameinfo.gi (passo a passo no README.txt do zip). Para remover, apague o arquivo.

Idioma: inglês por padrão. Troque em loja (B) > Deadlock Utils > Configurações > Idioma (English, Português, Español, Русский). Muda na hora.

Tracker: Configurações > Tracker, informe seu código de amigo da Steam (Steam > Amigos > Adicionar um amigo).

Avisos: substitui o hud.xml do jogo, então não funciona junto com outros mods que também o substituem. A Valve não publicou regras sobre mods: use por sua conta e risco. Código aberto: github.com/Oluizk/DeadlockUtils
