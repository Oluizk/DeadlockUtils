Feedback is very welcome! I'd especially like to hear about:

  • Counters: an item that should (or shouldn't) be suggested against a hero. You can also share your edited counters with a DU1: code.
  • Translations: wrong or unnatural text in Português, Español or Русский.
  • Map points: anything missing or in the wrong place.

Reporting a bug? Please include: mod version, the language you picked, what you did and what happened, and a screenshot if possible. If the HUD breaks, remove pak42_dir.vpk (or disable it in your mod manager) and mention whether the game had just updated. Detailed bug reports are easier to track on GitHub Issues.
