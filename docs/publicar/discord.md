**English**

**Deadlock Utils 1.0.0**: in-game HUD mod (Panorama, no overlay). Build and counters in a new shop tab, counter and recommended items while holding Tab (click to buy), big map on M with timers and toggleable points (healing snacks, bells, jump pads, Rem's tunnels...), points on the regular minimap, tracker on F6, counter editor with share codes, settings and hotkeys. PT/EN/ES.
Download: https://github.com/Oluizk/DeadlockUtils/releases/latest
Replaces hud.xml (not compatible with other hud.xml mods). Feedback welcome!

**Português**

**Deadlock Utils 1.0.0**: mod de HUD dentro do jogo (Panorama, sem overlay). Build e counters numa aba nova da loja, counters e recomendados segurando Tab (clique compra), mapa grande no M com tempos e pontos que liga e desliga (lanches, sinos, saltos, túneis do Rem...), pontos no minimapa normal, tracker no F6, editor de counters com código, configurações e teclas. PT/EN/ES.
Download: https://github.com/Oluizk/DeadlockUtils/releases/latest
Substitui o hud.xml (não funciona com outros mods de hud.xml). Sugestões são bem-vindas!
